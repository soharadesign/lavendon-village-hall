# Content audit and design rationale

## What the two existing sites contain

### Lavendon Connection

Useful material:

- Registered charity number: 300289.
- Broad list of suitable event types.
- Contact details and links to hire terms and FAQs.
- Important temporary-event licensing warning.
- Background on the hall refurbishment and included AV improvements.

Issues:

- A single long article mixes booking information, history and fundraising updates.
- The primary booking action is a phone number rather than the newer booking flow.
- Important practical facts are buried in prose.

### Lemon Booking

Useful material:

- Current address, contact details, facilities and photographs.
- Capacities, indicative prices and equipment details for the main hall, whole building and kitchen.
- Live calendar and structured booking request form.
- Activities, ticketed events and downloadable hire documents.

Issues:

- Seven top-level pages create more navigation than the amount of content warrants.
- Facilities information is thorough but repetitive.
- Home, activities and events divide the public-facing story across separate pages.
- The booking service's generic visual treatment does not establish a strong local identity.

## Proposed two-page structure

### 1. Home & events

Purpose: answer “what is this place, what can I do here, and what is happening soon?”

1. Clear venue proposition and immediate hire action.
2. Managed upcoming-events component.
3. Essential facilities summary.
4. Common use cases.
5. Address, contact and charity details.

### 2. Hire the hall

Purpose: answer “will it suit my event, what will it cost, and how do I book?”

1. Spaces, capacity and indicative prices.
2. Included equipment and accessibility information.
3. Date and event-type capture for a clear booking hand-off.
4. Link to the current live calendar and Lemon Booking request form.
5. Licensing warning, hire terms, safety checklist and FAQs.

## Visual direction

- Warm village character without faux-rustic decoration.
- Deep green, warm cream and restrained terracotta for a civic but welcoming tone.
- Large editorial headings balanced by plain, highly legible body copy.
- Real hall photographs used as evidence of the venue, not decoration.
- Strong information hierarchy, visible prices, clear calls to action and mobile-first fallbacks.

## Production recommendation

Keep Lemon Booking as the operational booking engine initially. The new website should own the presentation layer and send users into the existing live calendar/form. Store public events in a small CMS collection with title, start/end time, category, summary, image, ticket link and publication status.
