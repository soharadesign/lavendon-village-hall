One hall, many possibilities

Useful, flexible and properly equipped.

The main hall measures approximately 12.5 × 17.7 metres, with a raised stage, commercial kitchen and enough height for a bouncy castle.

  • Tables, chairs, crockery and glassware included
  • Wi-Fi, heating and audio-visual system included
  • Projector, drop-down screen and microphones
  • Accessible toilet and baby-changing facilities
  • Parking for approximately 18–22 cars
